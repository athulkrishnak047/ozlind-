## Prohibited use

Do not use Ozlind to:
- Commit, facilitate or plan unlawful acts, fraud, scams, identity theft or credential theft.
- Create, distribute or deploy malware, ransomware, phishing kits or unauthorized intrusion tools.
- Threaten, stalk, harass, exploit, extort or target people for abuse.
- Create or distribute child sexual abuse material or facilitate child exploitation.
- Facilitate violence, terrorism, trafficking or other serious physical harm.
- Invade privacy, dox people, conduct unauthorized surveillance or process personal data without a lawful basis.
- Impersonate people or organizations for deception, or generate deceptive material intended to cause harm.
- Circumvent rate limits, security controls, access restrictions or provider policies.
- Infringe copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity or other rights.
- Submit content that you do not have the right or authority to submit.

## User responsibility

You are responsible for your prompts, files, use of outputs and compliance with applicable law and third-party provider terms. Do not treat generated output as verified fact.
