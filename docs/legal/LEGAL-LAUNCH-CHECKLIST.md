# Ozlind legal and policy launch checklist

**Do not mark this checklist complete based on the existence of draft files.**

## Operator identity and contact
- [ ] Confirm legal entity / proprietor name and business form.
- [ ] Confirm registered/principal postal address.
- [ ] Create monitored legal/privacy contact email.
- [ ] Decide complaint/grievance contact and escalation owner.
- [ ] Confirm applicable operating and target jurisdictions.

## Documents
- [ ] Terms of Service reviewed and approved by qualified counsel.
- [ ] Privacy Policy matches actual production data flows.
- [ ] Acceptable Use Policy matches actual safety and enforcement capabilities.
- [ ] Third-party provider disclosure verified against contracts.
- [ ] Cookie/local-storage inventory completed.
- [ ] Refund, subscription and billing terms added before paid plans.
- [ ] Copyright/IP takedown process and contact implemented before accepting formal notices.
- [ ] Effective dates, version history and update process added.

## Technical truth audit
- [ ] Inspect chat, research, transcription and vision route payloads.
- [ ] Inspect Vercel logs, error reporting, retention and access.
- [ ] Verify Google/Gemini and Groq data handling, retention and training settings.
- [ ] Verify browser storage keys and clear-history behavior.
- [ ] Audit cookies, analytics, pixels, third-party scripts and external fonts.
- [ ] Verify temporary file/audio handling and deletion.
- [ ] Ensure user-facing policy links are visible from landing/workspace/settings.
- [ ] Ensure policy pages are accessible on mobile and keyboard.

## India and other applicable law
- [ ] Obtain review of applicable Indian data protection, IT, consumer, intermediary and e-commerce requirements as relevant to the actual service model.
- [ ] Confirm commencement dates, rules and current regulatory guidance at launch; do not rely on outdated summaries.
- [ ] Assess children's data and age-gating obligations.
- [ ] Assess cross-border processing and provider contracts.
- [ ] Assess grievance, notice, consent and user-rights procedures.
- [ ] Obtain jurisdiction-specific review for every market actively served.

## Launch gate
No legal document is “published and approved” until the operator identity, contact details, data inventory and legal review are complete. A repository draft is not a substitute for a live policy page, a functioning contact process or legal compliance.
