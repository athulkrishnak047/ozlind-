## 2. Data we may process

The categories below must be verified against production before this policy is published.

- **Prompts and conversation content:** text you send and AI responses returned to your browser.
- **Files and media:** files you deliberately select and submit for supported analysis, including supported PDFs, images and short videos.
- **Voice input:** audio captured after you explicitly activate microphone recording and submit it for transcription.
- **Local preferences and history:** the current product stores conversation history and selected preferences in browser storage on the device/browser. This is not an account-backed cloud history or cross-device sync feature.
- **Technical and security data:** hosting infrastructure may process IP address, request metadata, timestamps, user-agent information, error/security events and rate-limit signals. The exact fields, retention and access must be confirmed with the hosting configuration.
- **Support correspondence:** information you choose to send to the published support channel, if one is provided.

Do not claim that no logs exist until the hosting provider and application configuration have been audited.

## 3. How information is used

Information may be processed to:
- Receive your request and provide the AI response or requested feature.
- Route prompts and supported file/audio content to the relevant AI provider.
- Maintain browser-local conversation history and preferences.
- Protect the Service, enforce rate limits, investigate abuse and diagnose failures.
- Respond to privacy, security or support requests.
- Meet legal obligations.

Any analytics, advertising, profiling, product training or marketing use must be separately verified, disclosed and supported by an appropriate legal basis/consent where required. Do not imply such use occurs or does not occur without checking.

## 4. AI providers and other recipients

The current architecture may send request content to Google Gemini and/or Groq through Ozlind's server routes. The exact provider depends on the feature and routing configuration. Hosting and deployment providers may process technical request data.

Before publication, list the actual legal provider names, service links, processing locations, data categories sent, contractual role, retention settings and applicable provider privacy/terms links. Third-party providers process data under their own terms and policies. Do not promise that providers never retain or train on submitted data unless verified in current contractual settings.

## 5. Storage and retention

- Conversation history is currently stored in the browser's local storage on the user's device/browser. Users can clear it using the product's available controls or browser storage settings.
- The current product does not provide an account-based cloud conversation history or cross-device synchronization.
- Submitted requests are processed by Ozlind's server routes and relevant third-party providers. Server, security and provider logs may exist according to their actual configurations.
- File/audio retention, temporary processing copies, backup behavior and deletion schedules must be verified before publication and stated here with exact periods or criteria.

**Operator action required:** complete a data inventory and retention schedule; do not publish this section with unknown periods.
