## Contact categories

- Product support: feature problems, usability and account questions (accounts are not currently available).
- Privacy request: access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or data-handling questions.
- Safety/abuse report: harmful use, impersonation, threats or policy violations.
- Security vulnerability: suspected vulnerability; do not send credentials or real user secrets in public.
- Copyright/IP complaint: rights-related notice using the dedicated procedure.

## Internal handling requirements

Before publication, assign a responsible person/team, monitor each inbox, define triage and escalation, set response targets consistent with applicable law, maintain a minimal case log, restrict access to sensitive reports and document deletion/retention.

Do not publish response-time promises until the team can meet them. Do not claim 24/7 support unless it is actually staffed.

## India-specific legal review

If the operator or service falls within applicable Indian grievance, consumer or intermediary obligations, counsel must identify the correct role, grievance officer/contact, required public details and statutory response timelines. Do not assume that a generic support email satisfies those obligations.
